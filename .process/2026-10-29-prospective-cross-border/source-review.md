# Travel baseline source review

OurAirports publishes an airport inventory with coordinates, type and scheduled-service status; those fields do not measure country route connectivity or arriving seats. Primary data dictionary checked October 29, 2026: https://ourairports.com/help/data-dictionary.html . Public-domain terms and daily inventory update: https://ourairports.com/data/ .

OpenFlights warns that its route-data provider ceased updates in June 2014. A current download does not make those routes current. Primary page checked October 29, 2026: https://openflights.org/data.php .

Proposed primary travel baseline therefore requires a separately reviewed complete scheduled-seat export. No subscription, export or access denial is assumed or purchased in this setup.
