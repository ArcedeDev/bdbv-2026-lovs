## Scope and source review
The protocol is a proposed country-first-confirmation ranking comparison. It is not a new active forecast or approved inferential test. Missing data may be obtainable; the gate records unreviewed/unacquired inputs rather than claiming global unavailability. Hashes bind bytes, not source truth. No aggregate export, source list, candidate or public receipt is invented to make readiness green.
